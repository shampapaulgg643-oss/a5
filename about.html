Who we are

About Ratchet Violet

An independent, ad-supported guide for anyone who wants to understand mechanical watches, from curious beginners to long-time collectors.

Our story

It started with an open case back

The first time most people see a working movement through a sapphire case back, they ask the same question: how does all of that actually work? Ratchet Violet exists to answer it properly.

We are a small team of watch enthusiasts who got tired of guides that were either full of jargon or full of sales pitches. So we started writing the explanations we wished we had found: accurate, visual and written in plain English.

The name comes from the ratchet wheel, the part that captures every turn of the crown, and from the violet tones of the evening sky when the day’s winding is done.

Independent

We do not sell watches and are not affiliated with any brand, retailer or auction house.

Explain, don’t hype

We focus on how things work and how to care for them, not on prices, trends or market speculation.

For everyone

Whether your watch cost a little or a lot, the same principles of mechanics and care apply.

How we research

Careful, checked and corrected

Our guides are based on established horological references, technical documentation published by movement makers, and conversations with working watchmakers. We double-check facts, avoid unsupported claims and update pages when we learn something new.

Read our Editorial Policy to see exactly how we work, or contact us if you spot a mistake.

A note on brands

Why we keep it brand-neutral

You will rarely see brand names on Ratchet Violet. Mechanical principles are shared across the industry, and keeping our guides neutral means the advice applies to whatever watch you own and avoids any hint of endorsement.

Photographs are sourced from Unsplash to illustrate concepts, and any markings visible on watches in photos belong to their respective owners.
