Technical and care advice

Watches differ widely in design and construction. Our explanations of movements, servicing intervals and care routines are general and may not apply to your specific watch. Water resistance

Our water resistance guidance is a general interpretation of common markings. Actual protection depends on the condition of seals, crowns and pushers, and decreases with age. Have water resistance tested regularly before exposing a watch to water.
