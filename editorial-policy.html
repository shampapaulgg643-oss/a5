Editorial Policy

How we research, write and correct our watch guides.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Readers trust Ratchet Violet to explain watches accurately. These are the standards we follow for every guide we publish.

Independence

We do not accept payment, loaned watches or gifts in exchange for coverage. We keep our guides brand-neutral and focused on principles that apply across the industry. Advertisers, if present, have no influence over our content.

Research

We rely on established horological references, technical documentation published by movement makers and standards bodies, and input from experienced watchmakers. Where details vary between movements, we say so rather than generalising.

Writing standards

  • We explain technical terms clearly the first time they are used.
  • We avoid exaggerated claims about accuracy, rarity or prices.
  • We do not give buying advice based on resale or market trends.
  • Safety-related guidance, such as water resistance, is written conservatively.

Corrections

If you find an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date when we make a significant change.

Images and graphics

Diagrams and the live dial are created by us. Photographs come from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and are chosen to illustrate concepts honestly, with descriptive alternative text.
