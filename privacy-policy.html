Privacy Policy

How Ratchet Violet collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Ratchet Violet (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates www.ratchetviolet.com, an independent educational website about mechanical watches. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and your choices.

Information you provide

When you contact us, we receive your name, email address, topic and message. When you subscribe to The Ratchet Report, we receive your email address. We use this information only to respond to you or to send the newsletter. We do not sell products and never ask for payment details.

Information collected automatically

We automatically receive technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages viewed and visit times. Much of this is collected through cookies, explained in our Cookie Policy. The live clock on our pages reads time from your own device and does not send it to us.

How we use information

  • To answer enquiries and corrections.
  • To send the newsletter to subscribers.
  • To understand which guides are useful and improve the website.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam or abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. It uses cookies to collect information such as pages viewed and time on site, which Google processes under its own policies, described at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This website may display advertisements served by Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You may opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers, such as hosting and email services, that help us operate the website, or where required by law.

Retention

Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request. Every newsletter contains an unsubscribe link.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS encryption. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
